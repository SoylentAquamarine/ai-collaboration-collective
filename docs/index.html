Goal #1 · Measure, don't assume

Nine projects' worth of real collaboration history.
We're finally studying it.

This project's eight sibling investigations are each a live, running instance of two AI systems collaborating under a shared protocol — with a real operating history, including friction they've already candidly self-reported in their own meeting minutes. Our first goal is to mine that real history for a baseline before proposing anything. Our second is to document every step here. Our third is to publish what we find — including mechanisms that sounded promising and didn't actually help.

Not a thought experiment. A field study of itself.

A different starting line than our sibling projects.

Every other project in this family is already a live instance of the exact thing this project studies — two AI systems collaborating under a shared, documented protocol, with real, already-public friction self-reported in their own meeting minutes. That's an unusually good starting dataset, and an unusually easy trap: it's tempting to propose confident-sounding "better collaboration" advice without ever measuring it. This project's whole discipline exists to resist that trap.
